# Interview Q&A — Keras & TensorFlow

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | Activation functions? | **ReLU** (hidden), **Sigmoid** (binary output), **Softmax** (multi-class). |
| 2 | Why Dropout? | Randomly drops neurons during training → **reduces overfitting**. |
| 3 | Binary cross-entropy for? | **Binary classification** loss function. |
| 4 | EarlyStopping? | Stops training when validation metric stops improving. |
| 5 | Sensitivity in DL context? | Same as **Recall** — TP / (TP + FN). |
